Damped Harmonic Oscillation Calculator

This interactive calculator solves and plots the engineering equation for underdamped harmonic motion (decaying oscillations over time or distance):

y(x) = A · e-γx · cos(ωx + φ)

Where A is the initial amplitude, γ (gamma) is the damping coefficient, ω (omega) is the angular frequency (rad/s), φ (phi) is the phase shift, and x is the independent variable (time or distance).
