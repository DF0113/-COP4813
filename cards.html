HTML5 Drag and Drop Card Deck & Discard Pile

The HTML5 Drag and Drop API brings native client-side drag-and-drop capability to modern web applications. In this interactive interface, playing cards are dealt from a shuffled 52-card deck into the player's hand. Each card is an interactive image with draggable="true". Players can drag any card from their active hand and release it onto the designated Discard Pile drop target.

How to Test the Assignment:
  1. Click "Deal Hand (5 Cards)" to deal cards from the deck.
  2. Click and drag any card from your active hand.
  3. Drop the card onto the Discard Pile zone.
  4. Observe the triggered event: the card moves to the discard pile, a capture notification appears, the poker hand re-evaluates, and the timestamped event is logged below.
